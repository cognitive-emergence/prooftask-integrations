# Customer case outline — fill only with real, approved evidence

Do not publish an unfilled outline as a customer case. Omit unsupported claims.

1. **Situation:** what actual delivery problem the parties faced.
2. **Agreement:** what scope, completion criteria and review arrangements they agreed.
3. **Delivery:** which exact version and materials were submitted.
4. **Decision:** who was authorized to decide and what decision was actually recorded.
5. **Result:** measured effects, measurement dates and limitations; omit estimates presented as facts.
6. **Reuse:** whether a second actual delivery used the arrangement, and what changed.
7. **Public verification:** an existing approved public projection or permitted supporting reference.
8. **Disclosure approval:** both parties approve the exact final content through the supported publication flow.

Exclude private originals, credentials, personal contact details and confidential terms. Consent to a delivery is not consent to publish a case. An issued record establishes its recorded claims and integrity boundaries; do not describe it as an independent judgment of quality or evidence of payment.
